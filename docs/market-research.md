# Market Research: Virtual Allowance-Tracking Apps for Kids

## Executive Summary

There are several apps in this space, but **no single app fully covers the captain's requirements**. The market splits into two camps: (1) real-money debit card platforms (Greenlight, Modak, GoHenry/Acorns Early) and (2) virtual-only trackers (Bankaroo, Bomad, Kids Wallet, Tiny Ledger, FamZoo IOU mode). The virtual-only camp handles basic allowance tracking well but almost none teach advanced financial concepts like loans, credit cards, and interest in an integrated, progressive curriculum. The two closest matches - PracticeBank and FamZoo - each fall short in important ways. There is a genuine gap for a virtual-only, educationally progressive allowance tracker that teaches loans, credit, and interest.

**Recommendation: Build Eddy's Wallet.** The gap is real and specific enough that the captain's vision would not be well-served by any existing app.

## Methodology

Searched the web for existing apps using queries targeting:

## Gap Analysis

### What the captain wants vs. what exists

| Requirement | Best Existing Coverage | Gap? |
|------------|----------------------|------|
| Virtual money (no real currency) | Bomad, Bankaroo, Kids Wallet, Tiny Ledger, PracticeBank all do this | No gap |
| Parent mode (deposit, manage withdrawals) | Most virtual apps have this | No gap |
| Child mode (read-only balance view) | Bomad, Kids Wallet have dedicated child views | No gap |
| Cloud sync across devices | Bomad, Kids Wallet, FamZoo, Bankaroo all sync | No gap |
| **Teaches loans** | FamZoo (real-money side), KidoBank (unclear status) | **Gap in virtual-only space** |
| **Teaches credit cards** | PracticeBank only (questionable availability) | **Significant gap** |
| **Teaches interest on savings** | FamZoo, Bankaroo, Tiny Ledger, PracticeBank | Partial coverage |
| **Progressive financial literacy curriculum** (balance -> loans -> credit -> interest) | No app offers this as a structured progression | **Major gap** |
| Age-appropriate, educational framing | Most apps are functional tools, not educational experiences | **Gap** |

### The two closest competitors

**FamZoo** is the most feature-rich option. It supports virtual IOU accounts, parent-set interest rates, and mock loans with repayment schedules. However:
- It's primarily a real-money prepaid card platform; the virtual IOU mode is secondary
- No credit card simulation
- No structured educational progression - it's a tool, not a teaching experience
- Costs $5.99/month
- The UI is frequently criticized as dated and clunky
- It does not frame financial concepts in an age-appropriate educational way

**PracticeBank** is conceptually the closest match - it simulates credit cards with interest, savings with interest, and checking accounts, all virtual. However:
- iOS only (no Android, no web)
- Appears to be a small/indie project with uncertain maintenance status
- No loans feature
- No structured educational progression
- No evidence of active development or cloud sync
- Limited to basic simulation without educational framing

## Key Finding

**No existing app combines all of**: virtual-only money, parent/child dual modes, cloud sync, AND a progressive educational curriculum that teaches balance management, loans, credit cards, and interest in an age-appropriate way.

The market is split:
- **Real-money apps** (Greenlight, Modak) are polished and well-funded but require bank accounts and real currency - wrong model for the captain's needs
- **Virtual trackers** (Bomad, Kids Wallet) are clean and functional but only track balances - they don't teach financial concepts beyond basic saving
- **Educational apps** (KidVestors, Savings Spree) teach concepts but aren't ongoing allowance trackers
- **FamZoo** bridges the gap somewhat but is primarily a real-money platform with a clunky UX and no structured learning progression
- **PracticeBank** has the right concept but appears to be a low-maintenance iOS-only app without loans or educational structure

The specific gap is: **a virtual wallet that starts simple (here's your balance) and progressively introduces financial concepts (savings interest, then loans, then credit) as the child is ready**, all within a polished, cloud-synced parent/child interface.

---

## Recommendation

**Build Eddy's Wallet.** The gap is real:

1. No existing virtual-only app teaches loans, credit cards, AND interest in an integrated experience
2. No app offers progressive/graduated introduction of financial concepts
3. The closest competitors (FamZoo, PracticeBank) each miss multiple requirements
4. The real-money apps (Greenlight, Modak) solve a fundamentally different problem
5. Building custom means the captain controls the educational progression, UX, and feature set exactly

The captain's vision of gradually introducing financial literacy concepts (balance -> loans -> credit -> interest) within a virtual wallet is genuinely novel in this space. The existing apps either track money or teach concepts, but none do both in a progressive, age-appropriate way within a parent/child cloud-synced interface.
